# mimpi

Research notes for a desktop AI companion that runs local inference on a consumer
GPU by day and trains a shared DiLoCo + LoRA "hive-mind" adapter while the PC is
idle. *Mimpi* is Indonesian for "dream" - the companion dreams (trains) while you
sleep.

**This repo contains proposals and analysis only. There is no implementation,
installer, or benchmark here, and no claim is a performance guarantee.**

## Contents

- `proposal-gemini.md` - the original architecture proposal, generated by Google
  Gemini and reproduced unedited with attribution. It describes a Tauri/Rust
  desktop shell, llama.cpp daytime inference, an Unsloth/PEFT nighttime QLoRA
  worker, and a FastAPI coordinator aggregating compressed adapter deltas
  DiLoCo-style. **Unverified design idea.**
- `feasibility-review.md` - a separate, independent review of that proposal: what
  is established (DiLoCo exists and the proposal borrows its inner/outer shape;
  the named components are real projects), what is plausible but unverified (the
  VRAM and payload budgets), and what is optimistic or untested (the sub-500 ms
  checkpoint target, LoRA hot-reload, the privacy and Byzantine-robustness claims,
  and the open research problem of merging LoRA factors across heterogeneous
  clients - the combined variant is not the published DiLoCo algorithm).

## Status

Idea stage. The sensible first milestone, per the review, is a single-machine
prototype testing the two most consequential local claims: preemption and resume
latency, and peak QLoRA VRAM on the actual GPU. No federated infrastructure exists
or is running.
